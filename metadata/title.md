## Winner: Relay Fault

Short, technical and evocative. The title suggests an audible relationship between a persistent pulse and increasingly disruptive responses. It supports an industrial, dark electro piece whose repeated patterns gain meaning through changes in context and order.

The narrative seed is a relay network that continues switching under mounting load. Development should come from displaced accents, terse monophonic answers and purposeful returns. The coda should turn the switching figure into a decisive rhythmic flourish. Detailed motifs and the order list remain pending sample analysis.
